About Sokha Beauty & Wellness

Sokha Beauty & Wellness is a demo concept e-commerce brand created for presentation purposes for BizWeb KH client project C033. We imagine a curated marketplace bringing skincare, cosmetics, vitamins and supplements to shoppers across Cambodia, combining premium presentation with accessible nationwide delivery.

This storefront and its accompanying admin dashboard are front-end prototypes intended to demonstrate look, feel and user flows. No real products are sold and no real transactions are processed.
