Lead Dentist

Dr. Nam Chamnan, DDS, AIC, FICCDE

Dr. Chamnan Nam, the co-founder of Melvin Dental Care, is an experienced, skilled, and highly dedicated dentist with a passion for both her profession and business. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Health Sciences in 2007 and a degree in Business Administration from Paññāsāstra University of Cambodia (PUC) in 2009. She is also a Post Graduate Resident, Master in Orthodontics at the University of Puthisastra, Cambodia.

She has more than 18 years of clinical experience working in numerous private practices. Committed to staying at the forefront of dental advancements, she regularly pursues professional training and education both locally and internationally.

Orthodontics is her primary focus. From 2013 to 2015, she completed the two-year Progressive Orthodontic Seminars (POS) live seminar course in Thailand. Her dedication led her to become a POS translator for Cambodian courses until the present day.

Driven by a desire to tackle complex aesthetic and dento-facial orthodontic treatment, she advanced to higher levels of orthodontics, demonstrating her commitment to providing high international standards of quality orthodontic care.

Dr. Chamnan is also the local trainer and mentor for American Orthodontics (AO), helping inspire the next generation of Cambodian orthodontic practitioners.

Dr. Nam's practice embraces comprehensive dentistry, incorporating her gentle communication and experience with children and nervous customers of all ages, helping them feel less anxious from the moment they step through the door. She is also certified in advanced IV sedation, expanding her ability to help patients with dental phobia.
