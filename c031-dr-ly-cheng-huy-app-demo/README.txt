Dr Ly Cheng Huy — Clickable App Demo
=====================================

Client: BizWeb KH — Dr Ly Cheng Huy ("Health Talk with Dr Ly Cheng Huy",
188K Facebook followers, Medical & Health category)

WHAT THIS IS
------------
A static, clickable mobile-app UI prototype for a telehealth / health
education app. Pure HTML/CSS/JS — no build tools, no npm, no frameworks.

HOW TO VIEW
-----------
Just double-click index.html to open it in any modern browser
(Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No server or build step needed.

The app is displayed inside a realistic phone-frame mockup. Click through
buttons, tabs and links exactly as a real user would. A "Screen Index"
panel to the right of the phone lets a reviewer jump directly to any of
the 25 screens without clicking through the full flow.

CONTENTS
--------
index.html   - all 25 screens, styling and navigation logic (self-contained)
assets/doctor-profile.jpg - doctor headshot used across the app
assets/style.css / assets/app.js - source copies of the CSS/JS that are
    also inlined into index.html (kept here for reference/editing)

SCREENS INCLUDED
-----------------
Splash, Onboarding (3-slide carousel), Login, Sign Up, Forgot Password,
Home Dashboard, Doctor Profile, Consultation Intro, Book Consultation
(5-step wizard), Payment, Booking Success, My Appointments, Appointment
Detail, Articles List, Article Detail, Videos List, Video Detail,
E-Book Library, E-Book Detail, Purchase Flow, Subscription Plans,
My Library, Notifications, Profile, Settings.
