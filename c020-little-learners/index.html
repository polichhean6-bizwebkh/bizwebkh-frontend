Everyday Care for Your Child’s Growth

  4. 10:00 AMCreative Play and Movement
  5. 11:00 AMLunch and Rest Preparation
  6. 1:00 PMQuiet Time or Rest
  7. 2:30 PMAfternoon Learning and Play
