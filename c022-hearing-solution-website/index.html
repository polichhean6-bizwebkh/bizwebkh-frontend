Understanding hearing-aid styles

Different Styles, Thoughtfully Explained

BTE

Behind-the-Ear

A hearing-aid style worn behind the ear and connected to an earpiece.

RIC

Receiver-in-Canal

A discreet style with the receiver positioned inside the ear canal.

ITE

In-the-Ear

A custom-style device designed to sit within the outer ear.

CIC

Completely-in-Canal

A small custom-style device positioned deeper inside the ear canal.

The most suitable hearing-aid style depends on individual hearing needs, comfort, lifestyle, and professional assessment.

Hearing Health

Common Signs That May Suggest a Hearing Assessment

Difficulty following conversations, frequently asking others to repeat themselves, increasing television volume, or struggling to hear in noisy places may be reasons to discuss a hearing assessment with a qualified professional.

Simple Ways to Protect Your Hearing From Loud Sound

Listening volume, exposure time, and how often a person is around loud sound can all affect hearing. Lowering the volume and taking listening breaks can help reduce risk.
