5 Ways to Manage High Blood Pressure Naturally

Dr Ly Cheng Huy
Jul 18, 2026 · 6 min read

High blood pressure, or hypertension, affects nearly one in three adults in Cambodia — yet many people don't realize they have it until a routine checkup reveals the numbers. Left unmanaged, it quietly raises the risk of heart attack, stroke and kidney disease. The good news is that small, consistent lifestyle changes can make a measurable difference within just a few weeks.

1. Reduce Sodium Intake

Traditional Khmer cooking often relies on prahok, fish sauce and MSG-heavy seasonings. Try cutting back gradually and using fresh herbs like lemongrass, ginger and kaffir lime leaves to build flavor instead of salt.

2. Move Your Body Daily

Just 30 minutes of brisk walking, cycling or light housework most days of the week can lower systolic blood pressure by 4-9 points — comparable to some medications. Start small and build the habit gradually.

3. Manage Stress and Sleep

Chronic stress and poor sleep both elevate blood pressure over time. Simple breathing exercises, prayer or meditation, and aiming for 7-8 hours of sleep each night can meaningfully support healthy blood pressure levels.

If you have already been diagnosed with hypertension, never stop or adjust medication without consulting your doctor first. Book a consultation with Dr Ly Cheng Huy any time you have questions about your specific numbers.
