About Us

Who We Are

Islamic Medical Relief for Cambodia Humanity Association (IMR-CHA) is a Cambodian medical humanitarian association rooted in Islamic virtues, compassion, and social solidarity. It works to improve well-being and help build an equitable, healthy, and resilient Cambodian society with long-term sustainability.

Our work is carried out by volunteer networks of physicians, healthcare professionals, and youth. Services are open to the Muslim community and to all Cambodian people without discrimination, with priority given to the poorest, the most vulnerable, and communities in remote areas.

IMR-CHA operates in cooperation with national and sub-national authorities, religious institutions, humanitarian organizations, and philanthropists, and adheres to the humanitarian principles of humanity, impartiality, neutrality, and independence.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be an Islamic medical humanitarian institution in Cambodia, contributing to an equitable, healthy, and resilient society through the delivery of high-quality, professional, and sustainable healthcare services rooted in Islamic virtues, humanitarian principles, and national solidarity — for the Muslim community and all Cambodian people, especially the most vulnerable.

Our Mission

To deliver equitable and free humanitarian medical care and rapid emergency response to the impoverished and vulnerable; to promote public health awareness; to build healthcare human resources through youth training and the mobilization of Islamic medical professionals; and to forge national and international partnerships that secure sustainable resources for humanitarian healthcare delivered with quality, equity, and compassion.

Our Approach

How We Work

We bridge community health gaps across Cambodia by combining professional medical expertise, trusted local networks, and strong institutional partnerships.

1

Professional Medical Expertise

We mobilize licensed healthcare professionals and trained volunteers.

2

Trusted Local Networks

We work closely with the public health system, local authorities, and community leaders.

3

Community Outreach

Community screening, mobile outreach, health education, and preventive care.

4

Direct-to-Provider Assistance

Transparent, direct-to-provider financial assistance for vulnerable patients requiring urgent care.

✓

Monitoring and Accountability

All activities are documented and monitored to ensure accountability, measurable results, continuous improvement, and sustainable community impact beyond each intervention.
