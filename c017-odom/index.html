04 / THE ODOM STORY

Designed for
community in the sky.

ODOM's vertical master planning creates community nodes throughout the development. Sky Villages, shared amenities and outdoor green spaces bring moments for work, connection and pause into everyday life.

The architectural language draws on Angkor Wat and regional materials, translating a familiar sense of place into a contemporary city landmark.
