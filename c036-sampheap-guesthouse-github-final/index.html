Explore

Explore Stung Treng

A quiet riverside province along the Mekong, known for nature, local culture, and relaxed exploration.

Irrawaddy Dolphins

Stung Treng is one of the few places to see the rare Irrawaddy dolphins in the Mekong River.

River Kayaking

Paddle out onto the calm waters around Stung Treng for a closer look at the river and its islands.

Nearby Waterfalls

Stung Treng province is home to scenic waterfalls surrounded by nature, perfect for a day trip.

River Islands

The Mekong widens near Stung Treng into a maze of green river islands, best seen by boat.

Sunset by the River

The best time to enjoy the riverside atmosphere, with peaceful views as the sun goes down.

Stung Treng City

Stroll along the riverside promenade and take in the relaxed evening atmosphere of the town.
