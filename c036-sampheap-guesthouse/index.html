Explore Stung Treng

A quiet riverside province along the Mekong, known for nature, local culture, and relaxed exploration.

Mekong River

Enjoy the scenic view and peaceful atmosphere along the Mekong.

Dolphin Watching Area

A great spot to see the rare Irrawaddy dolphins (in season).
