About The Doctor

Dedicated to Making Health Understanding Accessible for Every Cambodian Family

Dr Ly Cheng Huy is a Phnom Penh–based physician specializing in Internal Medicine and Public Health Education. For over 15 years, he has combined clinical practice with community health education, helping everyday families understand their health conditions clearly — free from myths, misinformation, and fear.

Through his platform "Health Talk with Dr Ly Cheng Huy," he has become one of Cambodia's most trusted voices in health education, reaching over 188,000 followers with practical, science-based content in both Khmer and English.
