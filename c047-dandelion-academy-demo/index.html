About Us

About Dandelion International Academy

Dandelion International Academy of Education provides a supportive learning environment designed to help students develop academically, socially and personally.

Our approach encourages curiosity, participation and practical learning while helping students build confidence for their future education.

  • Caring, student-focused teaching
  • Hands-on, activity-based learning
  • Focus on confidence and curiosity
