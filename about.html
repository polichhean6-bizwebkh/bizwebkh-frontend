Our Mission

Developing Practical Technical Skills for the Workforce

MEP Technical and Skills Academy focuses on practical, hands-on training for students, technicians and working professionals across core technical trades. Our approach centers on workshop-based learning, giving students the opportunity to build real, applicable skills alongside classroom instruction.

  • Practical, workshop-based training approach
  • Courses designed around real technical trade skills
  • Guided instruction for students at every level
  • Flexible intakes to suit different schedules
